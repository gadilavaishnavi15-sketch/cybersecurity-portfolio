A little about me

Curiosity, tested hands-on.

Turning cybersecurity theory into practical skills, one lab and investigation at a time.

I’m a third-year B.Tech Cybersecurity student at Swami Vivekananda Institute of Technology in Hyderabad, focused on building a strong practical foundation for a future in security operations.

My learning centers on security monitoring, SIEM, networking, Linux, Windows security, web security and controlled cybersecurity labs. I enjoy solving technical problems, understanding system behavior, investigating security events in logs, learning security tools and building practical labs with a strategic mindset.

I take ownership of my learning through research, documentation, AI as a learning and problem-solving companion, hands-on experimentation, troubleshooting and practical projects—then validate what I learn in controlled environments.
